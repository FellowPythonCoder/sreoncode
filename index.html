sreon://privacy

How privacy
actually works here.

Plain language. Not a promise
that the internet can’t keep.

01

This page does not search.

The public website does not send queries to a search engine. Searching happens in the desktop app, which asks external providers only when you search.

02

Your preferences stay here.

This page stores your theme in this browser. It has no analytics script or advertising tracker. The hosting service may keep its own request logs.

03

A website is still a website.

Pages you open in Sreon have their own cookies and privacy practices. Sreon is not a VPN or an anonymity service.

A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING

Just the
straight answers.

Is this a whole browser inside a website?

No. This is the Sreon site. Searching and browsing happen in the desktop app, not in this page.

Can I search from this website?

No. A search box here would be a demo without a live engine on the public site. Use the Sreon app for real results.

Does Sreon have its own search index?

Not currently. The Rust engine in the app retrieves results from existing online sources. Sreon is open about that distinction instead of claiming to crawl the whole web.

What stays on this device?

This page remembers your light or dark theme locally. It does not keep a search history because it does not search.
